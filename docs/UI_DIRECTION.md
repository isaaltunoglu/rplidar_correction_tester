## Direction contract

THESIS: Make selecting, measuring and preserving LiDAR evidence a single local workflow. Measurement state and provenance lead the screen.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue navigation, warm paper work areas, dark blue plots and terracotta angular annotations. Locally served Manrope, flat separators, standard browser controls and tabular values.

STORY: The visitor identifies the source, selects a sector, inspects returns, runs a documented trial and exports its settings and evidence. Missing data never implies free space.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop rail and top acquisition controls frame a source notice, heading/source selector, four-column telemetry band, large polar plot and right sector editor. On mobile the rail becomes top navigation, telemetry becomes two columns and the editor follows the plot. The selected-angle ribbon and plotted wedge describe the same filter; finite page-entry motion settles before capture.

FORM: Field Research Station, candidate 7 of the recorded seven directions. Seed `5b3a139c`, corroborated by `.impeccable/questions/roll.json`. The original roll ran degraded: online catalog/quality-bar cards were unavailable, so no catalog board or approved comp exists. This is a delegated code-led app, not a comp-fidelity claim.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
